What Tabora knows about you, where it is kept, who can see it and how to delete it all.

In short

  • The browser does not watch you: it collects no statistics and sends us no site addresses, search queries or page contents.
  • Passwords, sign-in codes, bookmarks, history and tabs reach our server only encrypted. The key exists only on your devices, and we cannot read this data.
  • Mail is kept on our server, otherwise it could not be delivered. We do not read it or use it for advertising.
  • There are no ads in Tabora, and we sell data to no one.
  • You can delete the account at any moment: everything is erased at once.

What stays on your device

History, bookmarks, saved passwords, sign-in codes, wallet keys and settings are kept in the browser profile on your device. The password vault and the wallet are encrypted on disk.

The browser collects no usage statistics, sends no crash reports and has no advertising identifier.

The Tabora account

An account is an address at tabora.app and a password. The account server keeps:

  • your Tabora address and the date the account was created;
  • a check value computed from the password. The password itself never reaches the server and cannot be recovered from this value;
  • the list of your devices: the name a device gave itself and the times of its first and latest sign-in;
  • the two-step protection secret and backup codes, if you turned it on;
  • the backup e-mail address, if you gave one;
  • a counter of failed sign-in attempts, so that the password cannot be guessed.

The network (IP) address you connect from is not saved in the account database. It gets into the server’s technical logs: web server logs are kept for 14 days, the others are erased as the space set aside for them fills up.

Mail

The letters in your mailbox and your addresses for sites are kept on our mail server in Finland: without that, mail could not be received or shown to you on different devices.

We do not read letters, analyse them or show ads based on them. Only those who maintain the server have access to it.

Letters encrypted with OpenPGP stay encrypted on the server. The private key is kept on the server only in a form encrypted on your device. The public key is published so that people can write to you encrypted.

When you send a letter, it goes to the recipient’s mail server. What happens to it next is decided by that server’s rules.

If you connect a mailbox of another mail service to Tabora, the browser talks to that service directly from your device. The password of such a mailbox is kept in the encrypted vault on the device.

Sync

Sync carries between your devices passwords, sign-in codes, passkeys, bookmarks, spaces, open tabs, the history of the last 60 days, addresses for filling in forms, bank cards, settings and the list of extensions.

Every record is encrypted on the device with a key the server does not have. The server keeps the encrypted records and sees only the kind of data (for example, “passwords”), the number of records, their size and the time they changed.

For bank cards, the number, the expiry date and the holder’s name are synced. The code on the back of the card is not saved anywhere. Any group of data can be turned off in the sync settings.

Wallet

The recovery phrase and the wallet keys are kept only on your device, in the encrypted vault. They never reach our server, and we cannot restore them for you.

To show a balance and send a transfer, the wallet talks to public network nodes: PublicNode, LlamaRPC, Ankr, TronGrid, mempool.space and the nodes of Base, Polygon and Solana. They see your network address and the wallet addresses you ask about. The wallet takes exchange rates from CoinGecko.

Transfers on a blockchain are visible to everyone and stay there forever: that is how these networks work.

What the browser itself requests from the network

Some parts of the browser need data from the network. In such requests the other side sees your network address but does not learn which sites you open.

  • Lists for the ad blocker: EasyList, RU AdList, EasyPrivacy and Peter Lowe’s list. They are downloaded from their authors’ servers.
  • Tabora updates are downloaded from GitHub.
  • Mozilla services inherited from the engine: lists of revoked certificates and dangerous extensions, tracking protection lists, the list of video codecs, updates of installed extensions and the service for notifications from sites. The notification service keeps a connection that carries a permanent profile number.
  • Google services: the Safe Browsing lists of dangerous sites and the module for protected video. The OpenH264 video codec is downloaded from a Cisco server.
  • Search: queries and suggestions as you type go to the search engine chosen in the settings. In private windows suggestions are off.
  • The breach check of passwords runs only when you press its button. The first five characters of a password’s fingerprint go to the Have I Been Pwned service: the password cannot be recovered from them.
  • The agent works only if you have added your own OpenAI key. Then your task and the contents of the pages the agent works with go to OpenAI under your key.

Support

Requests at support.tabora.app are kept unencrypted: support staff read them in order to answer you. If you write without an account, we keep the e-mail address you gave and send it a link to the request.

Other companies

The tabora.app site has no visit counters and no ad networks.

We use the services of three companies. Hetzner provides the server in Finland. GitHub holds the source code and the updates. Requests to the tabora.app site and to the account server pass through the Cloudflare network: sign-in, sync and, in the Android app, mail as well.

Cloudflare sees your network address and can see the contents of these requests. Sync data is already encrypted on the device by then. Mail programs, the web version of mail and the support site connect to our server directly.

We do not sell data or hand it over for advertising. We can disclose data to authorities only on a demand that is binding by law. We cannot decrypt encrypted data.

How long data is kept and how to delete it

Account data is kept for as long as the account exists. You can delete the account in the browser (Settings, the “Account” section, the “Sign out” tab), in the web version of mail at app.tabora.app (account settings) and in the Android app (the account screen). You will need the password and, with two-step protection, the code as well.

Deletion happens at once. The mailbox with all its letters, the addresses for sites, the sync data, the support requests and all device sessions are erased. The address is never given to anyone else: for that we keep only the names of the address and of its addresses for sites.

In the server’s backups deleted data can remain for up to six months, after which it is erased there too. What is saved on your devices stays on them until you remove the profile or the app.

If you have forgotten the password, first reset it through the backup e-mail address and then delete the account. If that is not possible, write to support.

Your rights and how to reach us

You can get a copy of your data, correct it, delete it or object to its processing. Your letters and other data are available to you in the Tabora apps.

With any question, write to hello@tabora.app or through the support site. If the answer does not satisfy you, you have the right to turn to your country’s data protection authority.

Age

Tabora is meant for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect children’s data.

Changes to this policy

A new version is published on this page with its date. We will tell you about significant changes by a letter to your Tabora address.
