The account

You are responsible for what is done in your account. If you think someone else has signed in to it, change the password and end the other sessions in the account settings.

Creating accounts by programs and in bulk is not allowed.

The mail at tabora.app is meant for personal correspondence. It is not allowed to:

To protect everyone else we may limit sending from an account that breaks these rules, or close it.

Wallet

The wallet exists only in the desktop browser and is meant for people aged 18 and over.

The keys and the recovery phrase are kept only on your device. We have no access to them: we cannot restore access to a wallet, cancel a transfer or return funds. Write the recovery phrase down and keep it apart from the computer.

Transfers on a blockchain cannot be reversed: check the address and the network before sending. The transfer fee is charged by the network.

Rates and prices are shown for reference and may differ from those on exchanges. Tabora gives no advice on where to invest; the value of cryptocurrency can fall to zero.
